Task sequence

Process files in the following order

  1. IRP5Condense.rmd
    • Gives irp5.qs, irp5a.qs
      • a: Agriculture only
      • Industry selected at 3 digit level (animal prod, crop prod, forest)
    • Job panel (firm ID, employee ID)
    • Revised
      • Location information 2013 ==> 2012, if firm ID and employee ID match
        • Assumes no transfer between branches in this two-year window, or effects of measurement error are minimal when considering the increase in sample size
      • Rows of taxyear that are submitted as revisin of the previous year
    • Dropped
      • Rows revised at a later year
      • individual IDs that are not of natural person in all years (any ID == natural person at least one year, is retained), saving 42M+ rows
  2. IRP5HHI.rmd
    • Gives irp5M.qs, irp5Ma.qs, LS.qs, FAD.qs, fa
    • irp5 panel of jobs
      • M: Main place level
    • Establishment panel of HHI and FA
      • LS: HHI measured at Main Place, Loc Muni, Dist Muni, Prov
        • We use main place level sample irp5M by following the “same sample data sets”
        • irp5M data only holds entries with location information of Main Place
          1. location entries are complete & location is granular down to main place level, or, NARowsMai == 0L & LocGranularFirm == Mai
          2. location == NA for some rows & uniquely common locality level is main place
      • LSMa: Establishment panel of HHI, main place level and agriculture only
      • FA: FA panel of establishment-workers
        • Establishment level; Number of jobs, employees, FA jobs, FA employees
        • Employee level: Incomes, DofBirth, Job dates, gender
      • faa: FA panel of establishments, agriculture only
  3. IRP5MergeData.rmd
    • Main analysis: faa + LSMa
      • faa is the base of merge
    • Decriptive statistics shows anomaly in number of jobs reported, too many and too few in 2009-2012
      • This is because we are selecting on firms with location information
      • Unconditional number of jobs looks good for all years
        • Many NAs in location info in early years → loss of sample in early years
      • Computing FA does not require location information, so FA can serve as the base of estimation data
    • Three ways to proceed
      1. Use selective sample with location information for whole periods
      2. Add location (Local Municipality) information from new version of firm panel
      3. Use HHI from later years (taxyear 2013, which is immediately before the increase in MW)
  4. IRP5Impacts.rmd
    • Event study like estimation of job% on FAHHItime + FEs
    • Negative estimates on firms with small HHI
    • Noisy/bumpy estimates on firms with large HHI
